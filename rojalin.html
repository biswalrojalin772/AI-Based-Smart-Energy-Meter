        How the meter thinks

        Measure. Each simulated minute the meter reads voltage and current, then works out power as P = V × I × pf and adds P ÷ 60,000 kWh to the day's total.

        Predict. The forecast for each hour is the average of the same hour over the last 7 days, scaled by the weekly trend and by how far the last few hours ran above or below that average. It reruns every minute.

        Alert. A reading is flagged when it is more than 3 standard deviations above the last 30 readings and at least 400 W higher. A second alert fires when the last hour used far more energy than the forecast for that hour.

        The past 7 days are generated sample data, not real measurements. Switch appliances on and off, or inject a fault, and watch the readings, forecast and alerts respond.
